Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

In short:We collect no data. Everything stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Samla has no servers, no analytics and no advertising SDKs. We do not collect, sell or share personal data.

Data stored on your device

  • Block lists, schedules and breaks.
  • Block Forever settings and any unlock request.
  • Your accountability partner's name, and their PIN stored only as a protected hash in the iOS Keychain.
  • Delete it anytime from Settings › Account › Delete Account & Data, or by deleting the app. Lists that are locked right now, and Block Forever, keep their rules until they end.

Screen Time

Apps, categories and websites you pick are handled by Apple's Screen Time framework as private tokens. Samla cannot see which apps they are or how you use them.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

Samla works fully without an account. If you sign in, Samla stores only Apple's anonymous user identifier and the name you choose to share, on your device. You can stop this in Settings › Apple Account › Sign in with Apple.

Purchases

If paid features are offered in the future, payments are processed by Apple. Samla would only receive the subscription status needed to unlock them.

Contact

A contact email will be added before publishing. Until then, see the Support page.
