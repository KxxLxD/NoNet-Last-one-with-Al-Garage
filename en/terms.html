Terms of Use

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Important:Hard Mode and Block Forever are voluntary commitments that can't be turned off early. Choose carefully.

Purpose

Samla is a tool that helps you build healthier digital habits by blocking the apps and websites you choose.

Voluntary commitments

  • Hard Mode: while active, the list can't be stopped, edited or deleted, and the app can't be deleted.
  • Partner protection: while active, the list only unlocks with your partner's PIN. If the PIN is forgotten, the partner is removed after 7 days.
  • Block Forever: stays on until 360 days pass after you request an unlock, or your partner enters their PIN.

Service limits

Blocking relies on Apple's Screen Time and may not cover every piece of content or every situation. The app is provided “as is” without warranties.
