Support

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Answers to the most common questions. A contact email is coming soon.

Why can't I delete the app?

A list in Hard Mode or protected by your partner is active, or Block Forever is on. Hard Mode ends when the list ends. Block Forever ends 360 days after you request an unlock, or sooner with your partner's PIN.

What is Block Forever?

A free, permanent filter for porn, gambling and dating websites. You can also lock every 17+ app. To turn it off you request an unlock and wait 360 days, or your partner enters their PIN.

I forgot my partner's PIN. What now?

Open Settings › Accountability Partner and tap Forgot PIN. Your partner is removed after 7 days. Block Forever isn't affected and keeps its 360-day wait.

Blocking isn't working?

Make sure Samla is allowed to use Screen Time in iOS Settings. Blocking only works on a real iPhone.

How do scheduled lists work?

Choose Schedule under Time, then add days and times. Blocking starts and stops automatically, even when Samla is closed.

Are breaks allowed?

Yes, except in Hard Mode. Choose how many breaks you get per day and how long each lasts, then start one from the list's page or the block screen.

How many lists can I create?

The free version holds 3 lists. Premium, coming soon, will remove the limit. Block Forever stays free forever.

Where does my data go?

Everything stays on your device. Samla collects no data.
