# Security Policy

## Supported versions
Only the latest version of Samla on the App Store receives security fixes.

## Reporting a vulnerability
Please **don't open a public issue** for security problems.

Report privately through GitHub: **Security › Report a vulnerability** on this repository.
You can also reach us through the [support page](https://kxxlxd.github.io/NoNet-Last-one-with-Al-Garage/support.html).

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the iOS and app versions. We aim to
acknowledge reports within 3 business days and to share a fix timeline within 10.

## Scope
Of special interest: anything that lets someone bypass Hard Mode, the accountability
partner's PIN or Block Forever, or that exposes data stored on the device.
